Case study 1 · Laravel, Stripe, Shopify

FunnelBridge: order and subscription engine for 50,000+ customers

I proposed, designed and built FunnelBridge, and have run it in production since November 2022. It sits between a US merchandise business's sales funnels, its billing and its fulfilment. Every customer who buys through the funnels is billed in Stripe and receives their order through Shopify because of it.

50,000+customers billed and fulfilled
Six-figuremonthly sales flowing through it
~260,000webhook events in 30 days (2026)
Since 2022in production, 4 years

What it does

  • Classifies every order. Each product is a single item, a bundle or a subscription. Unknown products raise an alert and hold that customer's orders, so nothing ships wrong.
  • Creates Stripe subscriptions with a calculated first rebill date (a set number of days, or a fixed day of the month).
  • Consolidates Shopify orders. Paid orders are grouped per customer and funnel into one Shopify order with the right line items, shipping and tags.
  • Ships the right bundle on every rebill. When a Stripe invoice is paid, the customer's step in the subscription schedule decides which bundle goes to Shopify.
  • Recovers on its own. A catch-up job retries anything left unsent, and products sync from Shopify twice a day.

Results

  • Cut unsent invoices per run from 5,000+ to under 50 (2025).
  • Tracked down the root cause of duplicate Shopify orders (2025).
  • Upgraded the app from PHP 8.0 / Laravel 8 to PHP 8.4 / Laravel 12 (2025).
  • Moved API keys and settings into the database so the app could be cloned, then cloned it into two more instances (2025).

FunnelBridge is private client software, so there is no code or data here. I'm happy to walk through its architecture live.

Case study 2 · Shopify Online Store 2.0

M&H Living: Shopify Horizon storefront

I rebuilt this UK furniture retailer's storefront on Shopify's Horizon theme, as a freelancer through the DevCrabs agency. See it live at mandhliving.co.uk.

  • Product pages from metafields and metaobjects: sofa-set cross-sells, accessory bundle pricing, fabric swatches, seat-firmness upgrades and delivery lead times carried into the cart.
  • Cart and theme APIs: Cart AJAX API, Section Rendering API and line-item properties, built on Horizon's component framework.
  • Admin GraphQL automation toolkit (Node.js): dry-run by default, with reports and backups. Used to launch new product lines and redirects, and to set up and roll back seasonal sales across 160+ products.
  • Also: JSON-LD structured data, consent-aware tracking through the Customer Privacy API, and a prototype Shopify Functions discount app with unit tests.
